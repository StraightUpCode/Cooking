Why it works

Retard this dough 24–72 h in the fridge (2–4 °C / 36–39 °F) and slow yeast + bacterial activity has time to break starches into more sugar and produce more organic acids and aromatic byproducts, deepening flavour and improving browning versus a same-day rise. Keep salt at roughly 2% of flour weight and never omit it — it throttles yeast to a controllable pace and tightens gluten strands, so the dough ferments evenly and holds structure instead of slackening.
